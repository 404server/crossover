& Falsified if & Equal & Proportional \\

H1 & any microservice configuration has a significantly lower p99 than A at the lowest rate & \xo{verdict/H1} & \xo{prop/verdict/H1} \\

Table~\ref{tab:verdicts} summarises the three tests. Decomposition costs latency at low load (H1) and the mesh's
cost compounds with depth (H3), as predicted. The central hypothesis, that horizontal decomposition eventually
overtakes the monolith (H2), is falsified for an equal CPU budget: the monolith's capacity was four to sixteen times
that of the decomposed configurations, and no crossover appeared at any load. Allocating the budget in proportion
to demand raised their capacity relative to the monolith by a factor of \xo{alloc/gainmin}--\xo{alloc/gainmax}
but did not close the gap.
